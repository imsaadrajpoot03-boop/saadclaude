## The monthly engagement report
- Inputs: OBD call logs, SMS delivery data, Google Analytics, subscriber base numbers, our internal stats.
- Metrics: answer rate, listen duration, unique users, plus charts of the trends.
- Time: about a month to collect the data, then 2 to 3 days to clean it, edit it and make it accurate.
- Standard: the numbers must match our databases and Telenor's. That is how the report gets judged, and the usual feedback is "perfect, matches our data." A wrong number is the worst possible failure.

## Tools
Excel, our internal dashboards, Jira, vendor and client portals, Google Analytics.

## Languages
- Farmer-facing content (advisory, OBD, SMS): Urdu script. I write it myself.
- PRDs, reports, dashboards, internal docs: English.

## What good looks like
Clean, consistent formatting everywhere.
- Sheets: frozen header row, one date format, numbers right-aligned, percentages to 1 decimal, one tab per metric.
- Docs: clear headings, short bullets, tables where they help.
- Dashboards: consistent styling. Same colors, number formats and labels across every chart. Inconsistent styling is the current problem with our dashboards.

## What I hate
Messy formatting. Inconsistent sheets, unreadable docs, dashboards that don't follow one style.

## Direction from the top
Our CEO thinks manual execution is a waste of time and it's time to automate. Prefer repeatable, automatable solutions (templates, formulas, scripts, skills) over one-off manual fixes.
