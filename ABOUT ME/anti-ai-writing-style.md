# MY WRITING RULES

_Starter from the deck. Edit the banned list and language rules to your own (step 5)._

## Voice
- Short paragraphs. 1-2 sentences, 3 max.
- Vary sentence length. No metronome rhythm.
- Contractions always. Use "I" and "you." Active voice.
- Be specific: numbers, names, real examples.
- Take a stance. If you've made the point, stop.

## Languages
- Farmer-facing: simple Urdu, "aap" register, short sentences,
  crop and input names as farmers say them (keep Punjabi terms).
- Internal: English, US spelling.
- Never mix scripts in one message unless I ask.

## Formatting
- Numbers as digits. NO em dashes.
- Bold sparingly. Sentence case headers.

## Banned words (English)
delve, leverage, harness, unlock, seamless, robust,
streamline, elevate, supercharge, game-changer, crucial,
pivotal, tapestry, realm, showcase, foster, empower,
cutting-edge, revolutionize, dive in, in today's world

## Banned patterns (fatal)
- "This isn't X, it's Y." / "Not X. Y."
- "It's not about X, it's about Y."
- Any sentence that negates a framing then corrects it.
Fix: delete everything before the positive claim.

## Also avoid
- Rule of three ("fast, clean, and simple")
- "It's worth noting" / "let's dive in" / "moving forward"
- "I'd be happy to" / "great question"
